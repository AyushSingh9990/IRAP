# Navigation and workspace behavior

## Administrative accounts

Administrative-only accounts open the administration workspace after authentication.
They do not receive links to the applicant application wizard, personal document manager,
payment history, membership records, provider courses, article submissions, or public-profile
editor unless the same account also has a real member, training-provider, or organization
journey.

Administrative users retain access to their own notifications and account-security settings.
Users who legitimately hold both an administrative role and an approved or requested public
journey can move between the administration workspace and their personal account dashboard.

## Public navigation for authenticated users

Authenticated users do not see new-account registration links in the public navigation.
Public information pages remain available, while the account action directs administrators to
the administration workspace and other users to the personal dashboard.

## Mobile and tablet navigation

The public website, account dashboard, and administration dashboard use accessible navigation
drawers on phone and tablet layouts. The drawers provide:

- full-width presentation on phones;
- a wider panel on tablets;
- vertical scrolling instead of narrow horizontal menu strips;
- body-scroll locking;
- keyboard focus trapping;
- Escape-key closing;
- route-change closing;
- minimum touch-target sizing;
- fixed headers and independently scrollable navigation content.
